1. Who we are

RevYne (the Android app, the website at revyneai.com, and the customer review pages it hosts) is operated by Vistrow Technologies, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data we handle and why. It applies to:

  • Business owners who sign in to the RevYne app ("you"), and
  • Customers who open a business's RevYne review page through a QR poster or link.

For data about customers that a business collects through its review page, the business decides how to use it, and we process it on the business's behalf.

2. What we collect

From business owners

  • Google sign-in details: your name, email address, profile photo and Google account ID, to create and secure your account.
  • Business details you enter: business name, category, contact details, your review-page link and settings.
  • Google Business Profile data, if you connect it — see section 3.
  • Content you create in the app: reply drafts, posts, photos and profile changes you prepare or approve, and scheduled posts until they are published.
  • Device data for notifications: a push-notification token for your device.
  • An activity log of actions taken in your account (for example, "reply posted"), so you can see what happened.

From customers using a review page

  • The star rating and the words they type, and any AI-polished version they choose.
  • If they send a private message to the business: the message and, only if they choose to give it, their name and contact details so the business can reply.

Customers post their Google review themselves, from their own Google account. RevYne does not post reviews for anyone.

Technical data

Our hosting providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) for security and troubleshooting. We do not use advertising trackers.

3. Google user data

RevYne uses Google APIs in two ways:

  • Google Sign-In (scopes openid, email, profile) to sign you in.
  • Google Business Profile (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/business.manage), only if you choose to connect your Business Profile.

With the business.manage scope, RevYne reads and manages the Business Profile locations you own or manage, in order to:

WhatWhy
Read reviewsShow your reviews in the app, alert you to new ones and draft replies.
Post or update review repliesOnly replies you have approved.
Create posts and upload photosOnly posts and photos you have approved, including ones you schedule.
Read and edit profile informationShow your profile and save changes (such as hours or description) only after you approve them.
Read performance statisticsShow your Google stats and build your monthly PDF report.

Nothing is changed on your Google profile without your explicit approval in the app.

How Google data is stored

  • Your Google refresh token is stored encrypted (AES-GCM) on our servers so RevYne can act for you when you ask it to. It is never shown in the app or shared.
  • Business Profile content (reviews, profile details, posts, photos and statistics) is read live from Google when you use the app. It is not stored beyond 30 days.
  • For new-review alerts, we keep only the timestamp of your newest review, so we can tell when a new one arrives.
  • Drafts you write or approve and your monthly reports are kept in your account until you delete them or your account, except that any Google content in them follows the 30-day rule above.

You can disconnect RevYne at any time from your Google Account permissions page, or by deleting your RevYne account.

4. Limited Use disclosure

RevYne's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, we:

  • use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features of RevYne described above;
  • transfer it to others only as needed to provide those features (see section 6), to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you;
  • do not use or transfer it for advertising, including retargeting or personalised ads;
  • do not sell it, or use it to determine credit-worthiness or for lending;
  • do not use it to train generalised AI or machine-learning models;
  • do not allow humans to read it, unless you give us permission for specific data (for example, when you ask for support), it is needed for security (such as investigating abuse), it is needed to comply with law, or it is aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.

5. How we use data

  • To run RevYne: your account, review pages, Google profile features, stats and reports.
  • AI drafts: when you ask for a reply, post or profile suggestion, or a customer asks to polish their review, we send the relevant text (for example the review being replied to, your business name, or the customer's own words) to OpenAI to generate a draft. Drafts are based only on the owner's or customer's own input. Nothing is published automatically.
  • Notifications: to alert you to new Google reviews and private messages.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes. We do not make spam sales calls.

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who process it for us to run RevYne:

Private messages and contact details a customer sends through a review page are shared with that business.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account and business data: until you delete your account.
  • Google Business Profile content: no longer than 30 days; only the newest review's timestamp is kept for alerts.
  • Google refresh token: until you disconnect Google or delete your account; it is revoked at deletion.
  • Review-page entries and private messages: until the business deletes them or deletes its account.
  • Server logs: for a limited period set by our hosting providers.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app under Account → Delete account, or by emailing hello@vistrow.com. When you do, we revoke RevYne's access to your Google account and delete your account, businesses, review-page data, private messages and Google connection. See Delete your account for details.

Customers who sent a private message and want it removed can ask the business, or email us with the business name.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Google refresh tokens are additionally encrypted at rest. Access to production data is limited to what is needed to run the service. No method of storage or transfer is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you of a breach as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. Email hello@vistrow.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

11. Children

RevYne is a business tool and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
