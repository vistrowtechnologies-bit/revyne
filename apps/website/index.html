How it works

Reviews that sound like your customers — because they are.

No fake reviews, no copy-paste templates. RevYne just makes it easy for a happy customer to say so.

  1. Share your link

    Put your RevYne QR poster on the counter, or send your review link on WhatsApp after a visit. One tap opens a simple page — no app needed.

  2. Customer writes in their words

    They pick a rating and type a few words, in their own language. If they like, AI helps polish the wording — it never makes things up.

  3. They post it on Google

    The customer copies their review and posts it on your Google profile themselves. Prefer to talk privately? They can send you a private message instead.

Questions

Good to know

Does RevYne write fake reviews?

No. Every review is written by a real customer, in their own words, and posted by them on Google from their own account. AI can only help tidy up what the customer typed.

Do unhappy customers get blocked from Google?

Never. Every customer sees the "Post on Google" button whatever rating they choose. They can also choose to send you a private message instead — that's their choice.

Can RevYne post on my Google profile by itself?

No. Replies, posts, photos and profile edits are always shown to you first. Nothing is sent to Google until you approve it.

Do my customers need to install anything?

No. Your QR poster and WhatsApp link open a simple web page in their phone's browser.
