## Brand & Style
This design system pairs the tactile heritage of Indian craft and commerce with the refined polish of high-end editorial broadsheets. The aesthetic evokes quiet pride, craftsmanship, and institutional permanence, moving merchant management away from cold utilities toward an intentional, curatorial experience. 

Visual principles:
- **Atmospheric Warmth**: Built on natural parchment tones rather than sterile clinical grays, framing merchant portfolios with dignity.
- **Editorial Contrast**: Deep indanthrene-tinted ink grounding luminous marigold and baked terracotta accents.
- **Bespoke Framing**: Fine, purposeful borders reminiscent of bookbinding and registry ledgers.

## Colors
The palette evokes natural dyes, sun-baked clay, and ceremonial gold, tempered by deep midnight-indigo ink for high-contrast legibility.

- **Canvas Foundation (`#F7F2EA`)**: The base background across applications, simulating raw handmade cotton paper.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFDF9`)**: Pure parchment tier that provides soft optical lift against the base canvas.
- **Primary Terracotta (`#C2410C`)**: Used for pivotal calls to action, active navigation markers, and decisive transactions.
- **Secondary Marigold (`#F59E0B`)**: Used for celebration indicators, balance milestones, highlights, and secondary attention cues.
- **Structural Ledger Border (`#EADBCE`)**: Hairline boundary tone, anchoring layouts with quiet precision.
- **Deep Ink Neutral (`#1B1740`)**: High-density ink replacement for generic blacks, lending depth and historic authority to all typographic tiers.

## Typography
Typographic scale utilizes high-contrast pairings: dramatic literary serif headers anchor page hierarchy, while modern humanist sans-serif ensures legible utility for complex merchant figures, inventory tables, and transactional notifications.

- Headers adopt literary proportions with tighter letter-spacing to emphasize bespoke craftsmanship.
- Numeric displays and monetary values use tabular figures in `Plus Jakarta Sans` to guarantee alignment across ledgers.
- Small uppercase treatments apply strictly to category metadata and status markers with expanded letter-spacing.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column editorial grid for desktop and tablet screens, transitioning to a single-column rhythm with full-bleed bleed containers on mobile.

- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (<768px), Tablet (768px - 1024px), Desktop (>1024px).
- **Margins & Rhythms**: Page gutters relax generously on desktop to maintain broadsheet-style whitespace, keeping content readable and deliberate.
- **Bleed Compositions**: Hero photography and merchant story segments break out of structural containers to span 100% of horizontal viewport width.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system avoids synthetic, blurry elevation layers. Depth is achieved through tactile tonal contrast, subtle material transitions, and razor-sharp border outlines:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base canvas `#F7F2EA` serves as ground level; interactive panels and cards rise naturally via `#FFFDF9`.
- **Crisp Edges**: Components use a deliberate 1px solid `#EADBCE` structural rule rather than heavy dropshadows.
- **Warm Ambient Shadow**: Critical floating overlays (drawers, modal sheets) use a single sunlit shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 23, 64, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(194, 65, 12, 0.04)`.

## Shapes
Geometry is disciplined and architectural. Soft corners prevent clinical sharpness while preserving the structured nature of ledger books, certificates, and archival frames:

- Cards and surfaces utilize subtle 0.25rem radii.
- Interactive elements retain crisp 0.25rem to 0.5rem perimeters.
- Circular rounding is strictly limited to avatar badges and micro count indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Terracotta (`#C2410C`) background, white text, 0.25rem radius, inset vertical padding of 0.75rem, horizontal padding of 1.5rem. Hover deepens to `#9A3412`.
- **Secondary**: Surface-matched (`#FFFDF9`) with 1px border in `#EADBCE`, Deep Ink text (`#1B1740`), with a gentle transition to `#F7F2EA` on interaction.
- **Tertiary / Editorial Link**: Underlined text in `#C2410C` with subtle 2px baseline offset, accompanied by a trailing glyph.

### Cards
- Base surface `#FFFDF9` with a continuous 1px outline in `#EADBCE`.
- Internal card padding sets to `space-lg` (1.5rem).
- Metric cards display numerical insights in deep `#1B1740` with serif unit markers.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Fields adopt surface color `#FFFDF9` with `#EADBCE` borders.
- Labels sit above the field in `label-md` weight.
- Focus state activates a 1.5px border in `#C2410C` without synthetic outer glows.

### Chips & Badges
- Status chips leverage muted tints: Paid/Active features soft marigold wash (`#FEF3C7`) with `#B45309` typography.
- Standard metadata tags utilize `#F7F2EA` filled shapes enclosed with `#EADBCE` outlines.

### Lists & Ledger Rows
- Table and list rows are delineated by 1px bottom borders in `#EADBCE`.
- Row items align horizontally with body metadata, using tabular figures for all monetary balances.
