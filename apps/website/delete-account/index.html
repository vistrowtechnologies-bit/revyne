Account

Delete your RevYne account

RevYne is an app by Vistrow Technologies. You can delete your account and its data at any time — no questions asked.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the RevYne app and sign in.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: By email

If you can't use the app, email hello@vistrow.com from the Google email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my RevYne account". We'll confirm and delete your account within 30 days (usually much sooner). We may ask you to confirm the request to protect your account.

What gets deleted

  • Your RevYne account and profile (name, email, photo)
  • Your businesses and their review-page links and settings
  • Review-page entries and private messages from your customers
  • Drafts, scheduled posts, reports and your activity log
  • Photos you uploaded through RevYne (your profile photo and photos prepared for Google)
  • Your Google Business Profile connection — we revoke RevYne's access to your Google account and delete the encrypted Google token
  • Your notification (push) tokens

What is not deleted

  • Things already on Google — reviews written by customers, and replies, posts, photos or profile changes you approved — stay on your Google Business Profile. You can manage them directly in Google.
  • Limited server logs kept by our hosting providers for security, which expire on their own schedule.
  • Records we must keep by law (for example, invoices for any paid plan).

Deleting your account can't be undone. You're welcome to sign up again later, but you'll start fresh and your review links and QR posters will stop working.

More details are in our privacy policy. Questions? Email hello@vistrow.com.
