About us

Honest reviews, made easy.

RevYne helps Indian local businesses earn genuine Google reviews and look after their Google profile, without fake reviews, pressure or spam.

Why we built RevYne

For a clinic, salon, restaurant or shop, Google is the new shopfront. People check the rating and read a few reviews before they call, visit or book.

Most happy customers never leave a review. Not because they don't want to, but because nobody asked at the right moment, or it felt like too much effort. Meanwhile, some businesses get pushed towards "review packages" and fake reviews that break Google's rules and put their profile at risk.

We think there's a better way: make it easy for real customers to share their experience in their own words, and make it easy for owners to reply and keep their profile up to date. That's RevYne.

What we believe

  • Real customers, real words. Customers write their own reviews and post them on Google themselves. AI only helps tidy up what they said.
  • Owners stay in control. Replies, posts, photos and profile edits are drafts until you approve them. Nothing goes to Google on its own.
  • Every voice counts. Every customer sees the Google button, whatever rating they give. We never hide unhappy customers.
  • Respect people's time. No spam sales calls, no long contracts, and you can delete your account any time.

Who we are

RevYne is built by Vistrow Technologies, a software company based in Pune, India. We build for Indian local businesses first, and your RevYne data is stored on servers in Mumbai.
