============================================================

App name:

Current deployment:

Product type:
AI-powered review assistant for businesses.

One-line positioning:
RevYne AI helps businesses collect honest customer feedback, generate customer-friendly review drafts, create QR review links, and reply professionally to customer reviews.

Important positioning rule:
Never position RevYne as a fake review generator.
The product must always support real customer experiences, honest reviews, customer choice, and review-policy-safe wording.

Target customers:
- Indian local businesses
- Doctors
- Clinics
- Hotels
- Restaurants
- Real estate businesses
- Salons and beauty businesses
- Opticians and optical stores
- Coaching classes
- Car and bike service businesses
- Retail stores
- Home service providers
- Travel/transport businesses
- Event/wedding service businesses
- IT and digital service providers

Customer promise:
Turn genuine customer experiences into clear, useful, natural review wording and help businesses respond professionally.

Tone of brand:
Helpful, trustworthy, simple, modern, professional, friendly, local-business-friendly.

Avoid these claims:
- Do not claim reviews are posted automatically to Google.
- Do not claim RevYne manipulates ratings.
- Do not claim RevYne creates fake reviews.
- Do not promise guaranteed 5-star ratings.
- Do not promise direct Google review posting.

Correct Google review wording:
RevYne helps customers write and copy review drafts based on their genuine experience. Customers choose, edit, and post their review themselves.

10. AI REVIEW AND REPLY LOGIC
============================================================

AI provider:
OpenAI API through Supabase Edge Function.

Current model setting:
OPENAI_MODEL defaults to gpt-5-nano if not set.

AI endpoint:
supabase/functions/generate-ai/index.ts

Supported actions:
- review_suggestions
- smart_replies

Review suggestion inputs:
- rating
- customer experience
- selected service
- selected product/type
- depth: standard or seo_deep
- business name
- category
- city
- services
- keywords
- language
- tone

Smart reply inputs:
- customer name
- rating
- review text
- business context
- language
- tone

AI safety rules:
- Never fabricate visits, purchases, outcomes, names, dates, discounts, or service details.
- Use only provided customer experience and business context.
- Do not pressure users to post positive reviews.
- If rating/experience is negative, preserve the concern constructively.
- Return compact JSON only.

Review suggestions:
- Return exactly 3 suggestions.
- Standard suggestions under 45 words.
- SEO deep suggestions under 90 words.
- Must sound like a real customer, not an advertisement.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.

Smart replies:
- Return exactly 2 replies.
- Warm, concise, professional.
- Suitable for Google Business Profile.
- Under 45 words each.
- Do not offer compensation or operational promises unless already stated.

Rate limit:
AI function currently rate limits roughly 60 requests/hour per auth/IP bucket.

12. PAYMENTS
============================================================

Payment provider:
Razorpay

Current payment flow:
1. User clicks plan upgrade.
2. App calls create-razorpay-subscription function.
3. Function creates Razorpay subscription.
4. User is sent to Razorpay checkout/subscription page.
5. Razorpay webhook confirms subscription/payment.
6. Webhook updates Supabase profile plan and business owner plan.
7. App refreshes profile and unlocks plan.

Razorpay functions:
- create-razorpay-subscription
- cancel-razorpay-subscription
- razorpay-webhook

Razorpay plan env vars expected:
- RAZORPAY_PLAN_PRO_MONTHLY

- RAZORPAY_PLAN_PRO_YEARLY

- RAZORPAY_KEY_ID
- RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET
- RAZORPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET

Important:

profiles:

generated_reviews:
Stores generated review suggestions linked to business/review request.

smart_replies:
Stores generated smart reply outputs linked to business/user.

usage_limits:
Tracks monthly usage of review/reply generations.

support_tickets:
Stores user support tickets with subject, category, priority, status, message, admin_response, assigned_to, timestamps.

payment_events:
Stores Razorpay webhook/payment/subscription events and processing status.

analytics_events:
Stores client-side page/event tracking.

rate_limit_events:
Stores rate limit buckets/identifiers for AI, payment, and support protection.

Security:
Supabase Row Level Security is used.
Users should only access their own data.
Public review pages can read public-enabled business profiles and insert public review activity.
Admins can view support/payment/analytics queues where policies allow.

19. IMPORTANT USER FLOWS
============================================================

New business user flow:
1. User opens RevYne.
2. User signs in with Google.
3. RevYne creates/fetches profile.
4. Dashboard is empty for new user.
5. User goes to My Business.
6. User enters business profile and Google Review URL.
7. RevYne creates a public review link and QR.
8. Business prints/shares QR.
9. Customer scans QR and writes genuine experience.
10. RevYne generates review suggestions.
11. Customer copies chosen review and posts on Google manually.

Smart reply flow:
1. Business receives a real customer review.
2. Business opens AI Reply.
3. Business enters rating, customer name, and review text.
4. RevYne generates professional reply drafts.
5. Business copies and posts reply manually.

Upgrade flow:
1. Business opens Pricing.
2. Selects Pro, Pro Max, or Yearly.
3. Razorpay checkout opens.
4. Payment/subscription completes.
5. Webhook updates Supabase profile.
6. App refreshes current plan and unlocks features.

Support flow:
1. User opens Profile -> Help & Support.
2. User raises ticket.
3. Admin sees ticket in admin support dashboard.
4. Admin updates status/response.
5. User sees response.
